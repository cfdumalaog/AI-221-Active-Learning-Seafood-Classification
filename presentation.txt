 Simulated imbalanced dataset
The throttling of the images per class in the unlabeled pool is supposed to simulate a long-tailed
distribution. In reality, fish data will vary depending on the species where some are common and some are
not. This way, the model will be forced to learn from a heavily imbalanced dataset.

Active Learning vs. Random Sampling

· Both strategies exhibited a comparable performance as the labeled pool grew from the initial 90 samples to 190 samples.

· Active Learning demonstrated an upward learning trajectory.

· By Iteration 8, Active Learning achieved a mean Test Macro F1 score of 0.8107 (± 0.0127), whereas the Random Baseline
   only reached 0.7371 (± 0.0284).
